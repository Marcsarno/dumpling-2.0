Art credits

Thank you to the artists who make free game assets available.

  • Maple Lane daily play: fifteen original toy and garden prop models created in Blender for this game; original procedural toy sounds. Selected CC0 KayKit Character Animations 1.1 interaction, pickup, throw, use, kick, wave and jump clips are adapted onto the existing Arianna rig with proportion and coat-range corrections. Her mesh, skin weights and original 2048x2048 color and normal textures are unchanged. No Untitled Goose Game assets or animations are used.
  • Outdoor pond and school walk: selected Quaternius Stylized Nature MegaKit plants and rocks, Animated Cute Fish and fishing rod, KayKit Character Animations 1.1 fishing clips retargeted onto Arianna, and the Quaternius Modular Women Worker crossing guard. All CC0. Pond splash: Peludo, CC0, filtered and trimmed. Paths, architecture, pond, signs, painted ground and roof materials, meadow fringes and safety accessories are original game art.
  • School interiors: existing CC0 Kenney Furniture Kit, Food Kit, combined with original Blender school furniture, architecture, surface textures and signs based on user-supplied visual references.
  • Family dinners: pizza, taco and roast turkey from Kenney Food Kit, CC0. Palette embedded in lightweight models.
  • Sunny Pup: user-provided Meshy model, rigged for this game. Skeleton and Idle/Walk motion adapted from Mesh2Motion — CC0 for the source rig and animations. Model proportions/textures preserved; fitted weights, retargeting and grounding added.
  • Pug by Quaternius — CC0-1.0. Original model retained as a backup after the Sunny Pup replacement.
  • Dog Poop by J-Toastie — CC-BY-3.0. Original model retained; scaled, rotated and placed in the game. Runtime carrying and flush effects added.
  • Shovel by Kenney — CC0-1.0. Original model retained; scaled, rotated and placed in the game.
  • Bathroom Toilet Paper by Quaternius — CC0-1.0. Original model retained; scaled, rotated and placed in the game.
  • Hand Wash Bottle by MiniPoly — CC0-1.0. Original model retained; scaled, rotated and placed in the game.
  • Furniture Kit, Nature Kit Mini Market and Building Kit by Kenney — CC0. Runtime palette, scale and placement changes.
  • Crib by Poly by Google — CC BY 3.0. Original GLB retained; runtime pastel colors, scale and nursery placement.
  • Marc's retargeted motions: Universal Animation Library by Quaternius — CC0. Supplied animation_v2 revision retained; runtime walking alias and cleanup pose added.
  • Squishy Pop sounds: Interface Sounds by Kenney, CC0. Selected sounds converted to WAV with pitch variation. Music: Happy Adventure by TinyWorlds, CC0.
  • Squishy Pop sprite atlases were generated for this game from the user's supplied visual references. The arcade keeps its original sprite atlases separately from the 3D collectibles.
  • Chore motion: CMU Motion Capture Database, subject 13, washing windows and sweeping. Adapted and retargeted to Arianna. Free for all uses including commercial products; the motion data itself may not be resold. The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu. The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.
  • Classroom Blackboard and Bulletin board by Poly by Google — CC BY 3.0. Scaled, rotated and placed in the classroom. Classroom desks, chairs, books, storage and accessories use the CC0 Kenney Furniture Kit with a school palette. Dad’s chair is a CC0 Kenney lounge chair with the footrest removed.
  • House music: relax_background1 by joaquinton — CC0. Converted to MP3 for browser playback.
  • Resting flex: CMU 140_08, starting lying-on-back excerpt. Reduced joint flex and authored bed alignment/breathing; same CMU terms and acknowledgment as the chore motions above.
  • School people: Ultimate Modular Women and Ultimate Modular Characters by Quaternius, CC0. Student meshes, faces, hair, clothing, vertex-color palettes and blinking are original game art based on user-supplied concepts. They retain selected CC0 skeletons and Idle_Neutral, Wave and Interact clips, with custom chair fitting. The same students appear in the classroom and cafeteria. The cook reuses a casual model with a fitted chef cap. Downloaded from the FreeModels mirror.
  • House playlist: Happy Ukelele Island Surfing Theme by Tarush Singhal and Forget Me Not by Kistol, both CC0. Matched quiet levels, MP3 conversion and fades.
  • 3D squishy dumplings and hinged bamboo reward steamer: original Blender geometry, textures, and 26 matching portrait renders created for this project from user-provided visual references. Includes a lightweight closed model for shop displays. No third-party model is included in these assets.

Arianna, Lilah and Marc are user-provided character assets. Arianna retains her original mesh, rig, and full 2048x2048 material images. Lilah retains her original mesh, rig, 2048x2048 color and 1024x1024 metallic/roughness textures. Other game geometry and interface were created for this project.
